618 Mid-Year Shopping Festival 618 年中大促

June 18 started as JD.com's anniversary sale and grew into China's second-largest online shopping festival, with promotions running from late May through 6.18.

Dates 2026–2030

What 618 is

JD.com was founded on June 18, 1998. Its anniversary sale expanded across platforms and livestream commerce to become the mid-year counterpart to 11.11. Electronics, appliances, beauty and mother-and-baby categories lead.

How to plan for 618

  1. Register for platform campaigns 6–8 weeks ahead.
  2. Plan pre-sale deposits and tiered coupons; watch margin.
  3. Use 8-ending price points (e.g. 618 → 688) and never a 4.
  4. Book livestream slots early — top hosts sell out.

FAQ

When does 618 start?

Main promotions usually begin in late May or June 1 and peak on June 18.

Is 618 bigger than 11.11?

No — 11.11 (Singles' Day) remains the largest; 618 is generally ranked second.

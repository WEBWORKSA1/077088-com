8·8 — Father's Day (Taiwan) & 8.8 Shopping Day 八八节 · 爸爸节

August 8 — the “088” in 077088. 八八 (bā bā) sounds like 爸爸 (bàba, “dad”), so Taiwan celebrates Father's Day on 8/8; and 8 itself sounds like 发 (fā, prosper).

Dates 2026–2030

One date, three meanings

  • Father's Day in Taiwan — “Baba Day” (爸爸节) is marked on August 8 because 八八 ≈ 爸爸. It is widely observed, though not a public day off.
  • Double prosperity — 88 reads as 发发 (“prosper, prosper”), a favourite ending for prices, phone numbers and business openings.
  • “Bye-bye” — online, 88 is also shorthand for 拜拜. Context decides; in commerce it reads as prosperity.

The number's pull is real: the Beijing Olympics opened on 08-08-2008 at 8:08 pm.

8.8 as a shopping moment

E-commerce platforms run 8.8 mid-summer promotions, and it is a useful awareness moment for international brands between 618 and 11.11. Categories: men's grooming, watches, tech, wine & spirits, golf and outdoor, health supplements for parents, and “gifts for dad” bundles.

2027 twist: Qixi (7·7 lunar) also falls on August 8, 2027 — one date that works for both couples' and fathers' gifting.

8·8 campaign ideas

  1. “Baba Box” bundles priced at 88, 888 or 1,688.
  2. “Double 8, double gift” — buy one for dad, one for yourself.
  3. Launch a new store, product or number-based membership on 8/8 at 8:08.
  4. For Taiwan audiences, pair emotional storytelling with practical gifts.

FAQ

Why is Father's Day on August 8 in Taiwan?

Because 八八 (bā bā) sounds like 爸爸 (bàba), the word for dad.

Is 88 a lucky number?

Yes — 8 (bā) sounds like 发 (fā, prosper), so 88 means double prosperity. Online, 88 also means 'bye-bye'.

Is mainland China's Father's Day also on 8/8?

Mainland China generally follows the third Sunday of June; 8/8 is the Taiwan tradition.

Mid-Autumn Festival 中秋节

The 15th day of the 8th lunar month — full moon, family reunion and mooncakes. The number-one corporate gifting season of autumn.

· Plan a campaign

Dates 2026–2030

Traditions

Families gather to admire the full moon, eat mooncakes (月饼, round for reunion 团圆) and light lanterns. The legend of Chang'e and the Jade Rabbit is central.

Commerce

  • Premium mooncake gift boxes, tea, fruit, wine and baijiu.
  • Corporate gift orders are placed 4–8 weeks ahead.
  • Moon, rabbit and lantern motifs; red & gold packaging.

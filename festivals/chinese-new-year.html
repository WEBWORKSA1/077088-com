Chinese New Year (Spring Festival) 春节

The first day of the lunar year and the world's largest annual human migration. Red envelopes, family feasts, zodiac-themed products and the year's biggest gifting budget.

· Plan a campaign

Dates 2026–2030

Key customs

  • Reunion dinner on New Year's Eve; dumplings in the north, rice cakes (年糕) in the south.
  • Red envelopes (红包) for children, elders and staff — see the Hongbao Calculator.
  • Red decorations, couplets and the character 福 (fortune) — often hung upside down (倒, dào ≈ 到, “arrived”).
  • Zodiac year: 2026 Fire Horse, 2027 Fire Goat, 2028 Earth Monkey.

Commerce & marketing

CNY drives gifting (premium food, liquor, tea, gold), home goods, travel and new clothes. Zodiac limited editions are standard for global brands — but design them carefully: cartoonish or off-culture animal designs are criticised every year. Logistics slow down around the holiday; set shipping cutoffs 2–3 weeks early.

Taboos in the first days

  • Avoid the number 4, white/black gift wrap and clocks.
  • Traditionally no sweeping on day one (don't sweep away luck).
  • Avoid breaking things and negative words.

FAQ

When is Chinese New Year 2027?

Saturday, February 6, 2027 — the start of the Year of the Goat (Fire).

When is Chinese New Year 2028?

Wednesday, January 26, 2028 — Year of the Monkey (Earth).

How much should I put in a red envelope?

It depends on relationship and region; avoid 4s and prefer 6, 8 or 9. Try the calculator.

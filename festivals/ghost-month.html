Ghost Month (7th Lunar Month) 鬼月 · 中元节

The 7th lunar month is traditionally when the gates of the afterlife open. The Ghost Festival (中元节) falls on the 15th. Many families avoid weddings, moving house and big launches — plan around it.

· Plan a campaign

Dates 2026–2030

What to avoid (traditionally)

  • Weddings, moving house, opening a business or signing major deals.
  • Swimming at night, whistling at night, leaving clothes out overnight (folk beliefs).
  • Promotions that joke about ghosts or death.

Observance varies — strongest in Taiwan, Hong Kong, Singapore, Malaysia and southern China, lighter in many mainland cities.

How brands handle it

Keep running everyday commerce, but move store openings, weddings-related launches and property promotions before or after the month. Qixi (7th day) is the major exception — romance marketing thrives. Use the Launch Date Checker to spot conflicts.

FAQ

When is Ghost Month 2027?

August 2 – August 31, 2027 (7th lunar month). The Ghost Festival is August 16, 2027.

Is it bad to launch a business in Ghost Month?

Many Chinese-speaking customers and owners consider it inauspicious; if your audience is traditional, schedule openings outside it.

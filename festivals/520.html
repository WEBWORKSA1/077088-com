520 — Internet Valentine's Day 520 · 网络情人节

5-2-0 (wǔ èr líng) sounds like 我爱你 (wǒ ài nǐ, “I love you”). May 20 — and 5.21 — became China's digital-native love day.

Dates 2026–2030

How a number became a holiday

520 spread through internet slang and pop culture until May 20 became a day for confessions, gifts, marriage registrations and digital red envelopes of ¥520 or ¥5.20. Its twin, 521, reads as 我愿意 (“I'm willing”) or 我爱你 as well.

Marketing on 520

  • Younger, mobile-first audience — social platforms and live-streams drive sales.
  • Use love-code pricing: 520, 1314 (一生一世, forever), 5201314.
  • Categories: jewellery, cosmetics, fashion, flowers, chocolate, dining, experiences.

FAQ

What does 520 mean in Chinese?

520 sounds like 我爱你 (wǒ ài nǐ) — “I love you”.

What does 1314 mean?

一生一世 (yī shēng yī shì) — “for a lifetime”. 5201314 = “I love you forever”.

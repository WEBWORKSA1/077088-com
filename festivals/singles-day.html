11.11 Singles' Day (Double Eleven) 双十一

The world's largest online shopping festival. Four 1s (“bare sticks”) turned an anti-Valentine's joke into a mega-sale — Alibaba reported US$84.5 billion GMV in 2021, then stopped publishing totals.

· Plan a campaign

Dates 2026–2030

From joke to juggernaut

11.11 began as a campus “singles” celebration; Alibaba's Tmall turned it into a sale in 2009. Reported Alibaba GMV rose from about US$0.01 billion in 2009 to US$84.5 billion in 2021. From 2022, Alibaba and JD.com stopped disclosing headline totals.

Timeline for brands

  • August–September: platform registration, product selection, inventory.
  • Mid/late October: pre-sales and deposits open.
  • Early November: first sales wave; final peak on November 11.
  • Post-sale: returns, reviews, retargeting for 12.12.

FAQ

Why is it called Singles' Day?

Because 11/11 is four 1s, which look like single people (光棍, “bare sticks”).

How big is Singles' Day?

Alibaba last published a total of US$84.5 billion GMV for 2021; platforms stopped publishing headline totals from 2022.

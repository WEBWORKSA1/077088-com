Qixi Festival — Chinese Valentine's Day 七夕节

Held on the 7th day of the 7th lunar month — the “077” in 077088. China's oldest love festival and one of the year's biggest days for jewellery, flowers and luxury gifting.

The story behind 7·7

Qixi (七夕, “the evening of sevens”) retells the legend of the cowherd Niulang and the weaver girl Zhinü, lovers separated by the Milky Way who may meet only once a year, on a bridge of magpies. The stars Altair and Vega represent the pair. Historically it was also a day for young women to pray for skill in needlework (乞巧, qǐqiǎo).

Because it follows the lunar calendar, Qixi moves every year on the Western calendar — usually landing in August. In 2027 Qixi falls on August 8, the same day as 8·8, so the two halves of “077 088” meet on one date.

Why Qixi matters commercially

Modern Qixi is China's most culturally rooted gifting day for couples. Brands in jewellery, cosmetics, fragrance, fashion, flowers, chocolate, dining and travel run limited editions and couples' bundles. Luxury houses have used Qixi-only drops for years, and platform campaigns typically start 1–3 weeks before the date.

  • Top categories: gold & diamond jewellery, fragrance, lipstick sets, handbags, flowers, couples' experiences.
  • Symbols that sell: magpies, the Milky Way, paired stars, bridges, red strings (fate), and the numbers 7, 520 and 1314.
  • Price points: 520, 1314 and 5,201.314-style playful prices are common; 8-endings signal prosperity.

Qixi & Ghost Month

Qixi sits inside the 7th lunar month, traditionally called Ghost Month. Romance marketing on Qixi itself is universal, but some families avoid weddings and major life events for the rest of the month. Plan wedding-related launches for before or after — see Ghost Month dates.

Qixi campaign checklist

  1. Pick one hero product and a couples' bundle.
  2. Use a love-code price (520 / 1314) or an 8-ending price — never a 4.
  3. Build a limited edition with magpie, star or red-string design.
  4. Brief influencers 30+ days ahead; go live in the final 7 days.
  5. Offer engraving/personalisation and a guaranteed-before-Qixi delivery cutoff.

FAQ

When is Qixi in 2027?

Qixi 2027 is on Sunday, August 8, 2027 — the same date as 8·8.

Is Qixi the same as Chinese Valentine's Day?

Yes. Qixi is often called Chinese Valentine's Day. 520 (May 20) is a newer internet-era love day, and Feb 14 is also celebrated.

What should I give on Qixi?

Jewellery, flowers (red roses — avoid white or yellow chrysanthemums), fragrance and personalised gifts. See our Gift Taboo Checker.

Why does the date change every year?

Qixi follows the Chinese lunisolar calendar (7th day of the 7th month), so its Gregorian date shifts, usually within August.

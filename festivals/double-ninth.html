Double Ninth (Chongyang) Festival 重阳节

The 9th day of the 9th lunar month. 九九 (jiǔ jiǔ) sounds like 久久 (“long-lasting”), making it a day to honour elders and longevity.

Gifting

Health products for elders, tea, chrysanthemum wine (a traditional exception — for drinking, not as a gift bouquet), walking/hiking gear and travel for seniors.

3.8 Queen's Day / Goddess Day 女王节 · 女神节

International Women's Day (March 8), rebranded by e-commerce as “Queen's Day” or “Goddess Day” — a spring sale for beauty, self-care and fashion.

· Plan a campaign

Dates 2026–2030

Naming matters

Brands avoid “三八” (sān bā) in copy because it is also a slang insult; they use 女王节 or 女神节 instead. Self-care, beauty devices, skincare and fitness lead.

FAQ

Why not call it 38 festival?

Because 三八 is also slang for a nosy or gossipy woman, so brands say Queen's Day (女王节) instead.

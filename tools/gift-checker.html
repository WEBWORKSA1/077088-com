Why gifts carry puns

Mandarin has many homophones, so a gift's name can “say” something. 送钟 (give a clock) sounds exactly like 送终 (attend someone's death); 伞 (umbrella) sounds like 散 (to part). Taboos are strongest with elders, business partners and traditional families — and at weddings, New Year and hospital visits. Full list: Chinese Gift Taboos guide.

FAQ

Can I give a watch?

A watch (手表) is usually acceptable between partners, but avoid clocks, and be careful with elders.

What's a safe business gift?

Premium tea, fruit baskets, quality wine or baijiu (in pairs), or items from your home country — wrapped in red or gold.

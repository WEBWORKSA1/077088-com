Red Envelope Calculator 红包

How much money to put in a red envelope — by occasion, relationship, closeness and currency, rounded to lucky, 4-free amounts.

The rules that matter most

  • Never a 4 — 4, 40, 400 or 444 sound like death.
  • Even amounts for happy occasions; odd amounts in white envelopes for funerals.
  • 6, 8, 9 add meaning: smooth, prosper, lasting. 88, 168, 888 and 1,688 are favourites; 520 and 1314 for romance.
  • New, crisp notes; no coins. Give and receive with both hands.
  • Digital red envelopes on WeChat and Alipay are now normal, especially for CNY.

Amounts are guidance only — they vary by city, family and income. Hong Kong and Singapore “lai see” are traditionally smaller than mainland amounts. Read our full Hongbao Etiquette guide.

FAQ

How much do you give children at Chinese New Year?

Commonly ¥100+ for your own children and ¥20–200 for younger relatives or friends' children in mainland China — smaller in Hong Kong/Singapore.

How much for a Chinese wedding?

Often enough to cover your seat at the banquet, rounded to a lucky even number — e.g. 888 or 1,688 for close friends in larger cities.

Can I give a red envelope at a funeral?

No — use a white envelope (白包) with an odd amount.

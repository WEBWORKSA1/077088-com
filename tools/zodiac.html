The New Year boundary

The zodiac year starts on Chinese New Year, not January 1. Someone born on January 15, 1990 is a Snake (1989), because the Year of the Horse began on January 27, 1990. The finder uses exact New Year dates from 1924 to 2044.

Elements

Each year also has one of five elements, set by the last digit of the year: 0–1 Metal, 2–3 Water, 4–5 Wood, 6–7 Fire, 8–9 Earth. 2026 is the Fire Horse; 2027 the Fire Goat; 2028 the Earth Monkey.

Compatibility rules

Traditional pairings: Three Harmonies (三合) trines, Six Harmonies (六合) “secret friends”, Six Clashes (六冲) opposites and Six Harms (六害). These are folk traditions — enjoy them as culture.

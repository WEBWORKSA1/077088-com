How the score works

Each digit carries a traditional association (8 prosperity, 9 longevity, 6 smoothness, 4 death). The engine weights the final digits more heavily — endings are what people remember — and adds or subtracts for recognised combinations like 168 (一路发, prosperity all the way), 1314 (forever), 250 (fool) or 748 (“go to hell” in internet slang). The result is a 1–99 cultural-association score, not a prediction.

FAQ

Why is 4 unlucky?

四 (sì) sounds like 死 (sǐ), death. Many buildings skip 4th floors and buyers avoid 4s in phone numbers and plates.

What makes a phone number valuable?

Repeated 8s (8888), sequences like 1688 or 168, and endings in 8 or 9 command premiums at auctions and resellers.

Can I share my result?

Yes — the page URL updates with your number (e.g. ?n=168), so you can copy and share it.

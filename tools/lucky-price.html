Lucky Price Optimizer 吉利定价

Enter any price. Get the closest auspicious, 4-free price points for Chinese-speaking customers — above and below, with a cultural score.

How the optimizer works

It generates candidate prices near yours that end in culturally positive patterns — 8 (发, prosper), 88, 68, 168, 888, 66 and 99 — removes anything containing a 4 (sì ≈ 死, death) or 250 (二百五, “fool”), then scores each option with our number engine and ranks by score and closeness to your original price.

Is lucky pricing real?

Yes. A study of price advertising in Chinese markets (Simmons & Schindler, Journal of International Marketing, 2003) found a distinct tendency to favour the digit 8 and avoid the digit 4. Western “charm pricing” ends in 9 or .99; Chinese-market pricing often ends in 8 instead. Read the full Lucky Pricing guide.

Best practice

  • Keep 4 out of every position, not just the last digit.
  • Use love codes (520, 1314) only for romance moments like Qixi or 520.
  • For premium goods, round lucky numbers (888, 1,688, 2,888) signal confidence.

FAQ

What is the luckiest price ending?

8 — it sounds like 发 (fā), prosperity. 88, 168 and 888 are the classic lucky endings.

Should I avoid .44 or 14 as well?

Yes. Any 4 can trigger the association with 死 (death); 14 and 74 are also read negatively.

Does this work outside China?

It matters wherever you sell to Chinese-speaking customers — Hong Kong, Taiwan, Singapore, Malaysia and diaspora communities worldwide.

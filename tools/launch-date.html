Launch Date Checker 择日

Check a launch, opening, signing or wedding date: digit meaning, Ghost Month and Qingming conflicts, and nearby festivals to ride.

What the checker looks at

  • Digits of the full date and the month-day code (e.g. 8.8, 11.11).
  • Ghost Month (7th lunar month) — traditionally avoided for openings, weddings and moving house.
  • Qingming — a day of remembrance.
  • Days with 4 (4th, 14th, 24th) — avoided by some businesses.
  • Festivals within 21 days — warm-up windows you can ride.
This is a cultural screening tool, not a traditional almanac (黄历) reading. For wedding or feng shui date selection, many families consult a practitioner — we can refer you.

FAQ

Why avoid Ghost Month for a launch?

Many Chinese-speaking customers believe the 7th lunar month is inauspicious for new beginnings.

Is 8/8 a good opening date?

Yes — 8 sounds like prosperity; 8/8 is also Father's Day in Taiwan and a shopping day.

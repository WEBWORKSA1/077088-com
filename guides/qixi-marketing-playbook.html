Qixi Marketing Playbook for brands

How to plan, price and position a campaign for Chinese Valentine's Day — from concept to delivery cutoff.

By the 077088 Editorial Team·6 min read·Updated September 2026

Know the moment

Qixi is the 7th day of the 7th lunar month (2026: Aug 19 · 2027: Aug 8 · 2028: Aug 26). It is romantic and rooted in legend. For gifting it competes with 520 and Feb 14, but carries more cultural depth, which suits heritage and luxury storytelling.

Positioning that works

  • Legend-led design: magpies, the Milky Way, stars Altair and Vega, bridges and red strings of fate.
  • Personalisation: engraving, initials, custom packaging.
  • Limited editions: scarcity with a clear end date.
  • Experiences: dinners, stays and couple activities.

Pricing

Use love codes (520, 1314, 5,201) or 8-endings, and keep 4 out. Bundles in pairs are natural for couples.

Timeline

  1. T-60: concept, product and pricing.
  2. T-45: influencer contracts and platform registration.
  3. T-21: teasers and gift guides.
  4. T-10: live-streams and pre-orders; publish the guaranteed-delivery cutoff.
  5. T-0: same-day delivery, flowers and digital gift cards.

Pitfalls

  • Avoid white or yellow chrysanthemums, clocks and umbrellas (why).
  • Qixi sits in Ghost Month — keep ghost jokes out, and don't pitch weddings for later in the month to traditional audiences.
  • Culturally shallow designs get called out. Test your creative with native speakers.

FAQ

When should I start Qixi marketing?

Six to eight weeks ahead for planning; teasers about three weeks out.

Is Qixi only for mainland China?

No — it's observed in Taiwan, Hong Kong, Singapore, Malaysia and diaspora communities (with related festivals in Korea and Japan).

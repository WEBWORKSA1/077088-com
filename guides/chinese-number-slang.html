Work-culture codes

996 (9am–9pm, 6 days) and 007 (always on) are debated labels of tech work culture. They are fine in HR and news copy, but avoid them in brand copy unless you are commenting on them deliberately.

FAQ

What does 88 mean in a Chinese message?

Usually “bye-bye” (拜拜). In prices and numbers it means double prosperity (发发).

What does 666 mean?

“Awesome” or “smooth” (溜溜溜) — praise for skill, common in gaming and live-streams.

About 077088

A practical hub for China's number-days — built for the people who sell, gift and celebrate across the Chinese-speaking world.

Why this exists

Across the Chinese-speaking world, numbers carry meaning, and that meaning moves money. 8 sounds like prosperity; 4 sounds like death; 520 says “I love you”; 11.11 became the largest shopping day on earth. Yet most of what's online is either a trivia list or a one-market marketing report. 077088 connects the two: accurate cultural meaning, turned into dates, tools and decisions.

What we do

  • Calendar: festivals, mega-sales and caution periods, with lunar dates converted for 2026–2030.
  • Tools: lucky pricing, number scoring, Chinese numerals, red envelopes, zodiac, launch dates, gift taboos and campaign planning.
  • Guides: sourced, practical, and written for real decisions.
  • Brand Growth Desk: free campaign plans and vetted specialist introductions.

Editorial standards

We cite sources, separate tradition from evidence, and label folk beliefs as such. Our scores are cultural-association scores, not predictions. Corrections are welcome through the contact form and are fixed promptly.

How we make money

Advertising (clearly labelled), sponsorships, partner referrals from the Brand Growth Desk, and reader donations. Sponsors never buy editorial conclusions.

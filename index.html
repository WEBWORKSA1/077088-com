    The name

    Why “077 088”?

    077 → the 7th day of the 7th lunar month: Qixi, China's love festival. 088 → 8·8: 八八 sounds like 爸爸 (dad) — Father's Day in Taiwan — and 发发, “prosper, prosper”.

    2027 coincidence: Qixi falls on August 8, 2027 — 7·7 and 8·8 on the same day.

    What is 077088.com?

    A free hub for China's number-day calendar — festivals like Qixi (7·7) and 8·8, mega-sales like 618 and 11.11 — with lucky-number tools and guides for sellers, marketers and gift-givers.

    Why is it called 077088?

    077 points to the 7th day of the 7th lunar month (Qixi, Chinese Valentine's Day) and 088 to 8·8 (Father's Day in Taiwan and a shopping day; 88 also reads as “prosper, prosper”). In 2027 both fall on August 8. Full decode →

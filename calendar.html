How dates are calculated

Lunar festivals (Chinese New Year, Lantern, Dragon Boat, Qixi, Mid-Autumn, Double Ninth, Ghost Month) are converted from the Chinese lunisolar calendar. Qingming follows the solar term when the sun reaches 15° ecliptic longitude. Shopping festivals use fixed Gregorian dates, though platforms often start promotions days or weeks earlier. Official public-holiday arrangements (make-up working days) are announced each year by local governments.

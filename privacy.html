Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Form submissions (name, email, company and the details you type) — used only to answer your request, send what you asked for, or process your pledge or application.
  • Preferences stored in your browser (theme, cookie choice), which never leave your device.
  • Analytics and advertising cookies — only if Google Analytics or AdSense are enabled on the site, and subject to your consent where required.

How forms are delivered

Forms are sent through a form-delivery service (FormSubmit) to our private inbox. We do not sell your data. We share brief details with a partner only when you ask to be introduced.

Advertising

When enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we use a consent mechanism before personalised ads.

YouTube

Videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data at any time via the contact form. Newsletter emails include an unsubscribe option.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Retention

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to respond and for reasonable business records, then delete them.
